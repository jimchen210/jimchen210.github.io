1. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

1.1 What Information We Collect

We do not actively collect any of your personal information.

When using the App, you need to voluntarily enter the following information to generate a chart and analysis report. This information is stored only on your device (using Apple's Core Data framework) and is never uploaded to any cloud server:

  • Name: A label you assign to the chart for easy identification and management.
  • Gender.
  • Birth date: Supports either the Gregorian or Lunar calendar.
  • Birth longitude (optional): Used only when you enable the "True Solar Time" feature to calculate a time correction. This value is manually entered by you and is used only for local calculation; it is never uploaded.

1.2 How We Use This Information

  • The information you enter is used solely to generate, on your device locally, the Four Pillars chart, pattern analysis, Blind School operation system interpretation, and psychological comprehensive report.
  • All analysis and computation are performed locally on your device and are never transmitted to any external server.
  • We will not use your information for any other purpose.

1.3 Device Permissions We Request

1.4 Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. All features operate without external services, and your data is not shared with any third party.

2. Data Storage and Protection

2.1 Storage Method

  • All chart data and analysis reports are stored in your device's local database.
  • You can export your data as a JSON file using the "Export Backup (JSON)" feature. This file is also stored only on your device.
  • You can permanently delete all local data at any time using the "Clear All Data" feature.
  • We do not transfer your data across borders.

2.2 Data Retention Period

  • Your data remains on your device until you actively delete it.
  • When you delete the App or clear the data, all local data is permanently deleted.

2.3 Data Security Measures

  • Data is stored in the secure sandbox provided by the iOS system, which cannot be accessed by other apps.
  • No connection to any third-party server, eliminating the risk of data leakage during network transmission.
  • We adopt industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information.

3. How We Share, Transfer, and Publicly Disclose Your Personal Information

We will not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information in any form.

Since the data is stored only on your device, we technically cannot access, view, or share your data.

Exceptions to Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure

In the following circumstances, sharing, transferring, or publicly disclosing your personal information does not require your prior authorization:

  • In connection with national security or defense;
  • In connection with public safety, public health, or major public interests;
  • In connection with criminal investigation, prosecution, trial, and judgment execution;
  • To protect the vital legal rights and interests of you or other individuals, where obtaining consent is difficult;
  • Personal information you have publicly disclosed yourself;
  • Personal information collected from legally publicly disclosed sources, such as lawful news reports or government information disclosure channels.

4. How You Can Manage Your Personal Information

We take your management of personal information seriously and are committed to protecting your rights to view, modify, and delete your information.

  • View: You can view all added charts on the "My" page.
  • Modify: You can tap a specific chart on the "My" page to modify the name, gender, birth date, and other information.
  • Delete: You can swipe left on a single chart on the "My" page to delete it, or permanently delete all data via "Settings" → "Data Backup & Restore" → "Clear All Data".
  • Export Data: You can export all chart data (in JSON format) via "Settings" → "Data Backup & Restore" → "Export Backup (JSON)". This file is stored only on your device.
  • Export Reports: You can export the generated analysis reports as PDF files within the App. These files are stored only on your device for your personal review or sharing.
  • Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw all authorizations by uninstalling the App. After uninstalling, all local data will be permanently deleted.

5. Protection of Minors' Personal Information

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a minor between 13 and 18 years old, we recommend using the App under the guidance of your parents or legal guardians.

If we discover that we have collected a minor's personal information without verifiable guardian consent, we will immediately initiate the deletion procedure and complete the deletion within a reasonable period.
